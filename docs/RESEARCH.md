# Research retained in this distribution

Frozen V3, the Hybrid V4 challenger and their model specifications are retained.
This cleanup does not retune their weights, change the primary-printing universe,
fill historical gaps, or claim new predictive performance.

Current research entry points include frozen V3 evaluation, fixed Hybrid V4
comparison, EX/e-Card transfer evaluation, V4 regime research and prospective
30/90-day lockbox collection. Their historical observations belong in a local
PostgreSQL database, not this repository. Generated outputs remain gitignored.

The earlier weight-fitting, V2 exploration and V3 exploratory fitting programs
are not runtime dependencies. Their two still-needed history helpers were moved
without changing their calculations into `services/research/history.py`.
Original development code remains in the untouched private project.

Historical development metrics are not unseen future performance. The frozen
lockbox should be evaluated only as outcomes mature. A library being tested is
not proof that its model has predictive economic value. This public package
ships no historical-result dataset or calibrated performance dashboard.
